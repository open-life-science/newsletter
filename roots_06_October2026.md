Roots is your reminder that the world isn't going to change on its own, so we want to encourage and empower you to contribute to the change you want to see. 

Because we all can do something. *Some thing*. And that thing can be the seed of a growing huge tree, or a small plant.

But, remember:

***Nothing grows without roots*.**

***What's new in OLS:***

**🌱 🌳 New cohort programme launches in October 🌱 🌳**

We are exited to announce [Seeds to Systems](https://we-are-ols.org/seeds-to-systems/), our new cohort-based training and mentoring programme designed for individuals and teams leading open initiatives, that centres on organisational infrastructure for project and community sustainability.

Interested in learning more?  
🗞️ Read our [announcement post](https://we-are-ols.org/posts/2026/06/10/announcing-the-seeds-to-system-training-pilot-cohort/)

# Take part on the PREreview Review-a-thon!

[PREreview](https://prereview.org/) is hosting a **global, collaborative Review-a-thon** to celebrate [Peer Review Week](https://peerreviewweek.net/) (14-18 September, 2026)

Gather your lab, classroom, department, or community to review a preprint or dataset as a team. You can win a prize to support your Club's future activities! Registrations are now open through August 31.

[Read it here](https://sites.google.com/prereview.org/review-a-thon/about)
