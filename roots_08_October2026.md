# Register for our next Ally Skills workshop

***Our Ally Skill workshop is open for registrations - and it’s free to attend!***

Understanding and removing potential barriers to participation for different members, especially with less privileges than others is an essential step to support inclusivity in research.

We are offering a 3 hour safe space and interactive virtual training session, designed to **equip leaders with practical skills** they can build through group discussions, accessible resources and inclusive facilitation.

> *"\[...] the Ally Skills Training was truly amazing and informative. I learned a lot about myself as well as about allyship—insights I believe are both timely and necessary." (Kook Hu, past workshop participant)*
> 
> The next workshop will happen on [**October 14th, 2026 at 12pm UTC**](https://dateful.com/convert/utc?t=12p.m.&d=2026-10-14 "check the time on your timezone").

[**Learn more and register here!**](https://events.humanitix.com/ally-skills-workshop-october-edition)
