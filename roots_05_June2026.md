# We are reading... The Culture Map
One of the books I’ve recommended the most often in my life is “The Culture Map” by Erin Meyer. I always keep it in reach of my desk. The book analyses cultural differences across 8 scales: communicating, evaluating, persuading, leading, deciding, trusting, disagreeing, and scheduling.

Before reading the book, I thought my years of working internationally (away from the country that socialised me in international organizations and always in collaboration with people from across the world) made me good at this. The book opened my eyes though: suddenly I realized that most of past work conflicts and frustrations were down to cultural differences and the lack of social glue in remote-only collaborations. The book is mainly focused on countries from the global North and I wish there would be additional countries added. 

It turns out most things come down to the fact that you can take the girl out of Germany, but you can’t take Germany out of the girl.

Patricia Herterich
